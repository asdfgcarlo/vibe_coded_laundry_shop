Are these the shop’s final prices? {{ icon('plus') }}

No. All rates shown here are sample rates. Your final price depends on actual laundry weight, item requirements, and rates confirmed by the shop. Any pickup or delivery fee is confirmed separately.

Is my pickup confirmed when I submit? {{ icon('plus') }}

A saved request means your details have been received by the website. It does not reserve a pickup slot. The shop needs to confirm your preferred date and time, service area, and final arrangements.

How long does the laundry take? {{ icon('plus') }}

Turnaround times have not been provided yet. Please confirm the cleaning and delivery schedule with the shop before your pickup.

What areas do you collect from? {{ icon('plus') }}

The pickup and delivery service area is still to be confirmed. Share your full address in your request so the shop can check availability.

What if I don’t know my laundry’s weight? {{ icon('plus') }}

An approximate weight is fine for a request. The calculator gives a sample estimate; the shop will weigh the laundry and confirm the cost. Enter a weight from 0.5 to 100 kg.

Can I include special care instructions? {{ icon('plus') }}

Yes. Add them to the optional instructions field, including any care label concerns. The shop must confirm whether it can handle delicate, specialist, or unusual items.
