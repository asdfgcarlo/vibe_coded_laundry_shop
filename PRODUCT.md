# Dina’s Laundry Shop

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User-specified: HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript, Python Flask with Jinja, and MySQL. Local runnable project; no replacement framework or database.

## Product Purpose

Introduce Dina’s Laundry Shop, explain services and sample prices, estimate a laundry cost, and securely save pickup requests. A saved request is not a confirmed pickup appointment.

## Users

Customers requesting a laundry pickup. Inferred context: customers in the Philippines, based on the required Philippine peso pricing; exact location and service area remain unknown.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Wash, dry and fold; ironing; bedding. Calculator uses service and weight. Requests collect name, phone, address, service, estimated weight, preferred date and time, and optional instructions. JavaScript and server validation, CSRF protection, parameterized MySQL persistence, and unique references are required. Success is shown only after a successful commit. No customer data is publicly retrievable.

## Brand Commitments

Dina’s Laundry Shop. Fresh and welcoming; teal, cream, and dark navy. Original laundry/water SVG logo, standalone file, favicon. Responsive typography and generous space.

## Evidence on Hand

Only the user’s project brief. No verified rates, shop address, contact number, email, business hours, service area, reviews, turnaround promises, or delivery fees. Clearly label sample rates and placeholders; do not invent proof or business claims.

## Product Principles

- Make the next action understandable.
- Keep estimates and pickup preferences distinct from confirmed rates and appointments.
- Preserve customer privacy and never simulate a saved request.
- Keep the project maintainable and locally runnable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Accessible contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic navigation and forms, mobile/tablet/desktop layouts, and reduced-motion support.
